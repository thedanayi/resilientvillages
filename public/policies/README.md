# Resilient Villages Zimbabwe - Downloadable Policy PDFs

Place your official PDF policy documents in this folder (`/public/policies/`):

1. `pseah-policy.pdf` - Prevention of Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassment Policy
2. `anti-fraud-policy.pdf` - Anti-Fraud, Money Laundering, Corruption & Bribery Policy
3. `child-protection-policy.pdf` - Child Protection Policy (PVO 74/20 compliant)
4. `safeguarding-policy.pdf` - General Safeguarding Policy & Code of Conduct

When uploaded, these files will be directly accessible at:
- `https://your-domain/policies/pseah-policy.pdf`
- `https://your-domain/policies/anti-fraud-policy.pdf`
- `https://your-domain/policies/child-protection-policy.pdf`
- `https://your-domain/policies/safeguarding-policy.pdf`
